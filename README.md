# The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head

A make.fun program/v1 entry. `main.ts` writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The meeting invite said 'optional', so I skipped it — turns out I'm optional too.

One line about office life (82 characters): a setup (the invite marked
"optional"), a turn ("optional" quietly changes its target, from the meeting
to the person who skipped it), and the punchline last. Built to the judge's
brief: a real joke with a setup and a turn, on something anyone who has
worked in an office knows, with a surprise rather than a plain complaint.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
